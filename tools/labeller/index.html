InterCycle labeller

Images

    CAL points

    Click 4 (or more) floor points. For each, type its floor coordinates in metres and/or a relation to other points (e.g. "p2 is 3.0 m from p1 along the yellow stripe"). Drag a point to move it.

    #u, v (px)x my m

    Boxes

    Drag on empty image to draw. Click a box to select; drag it to move, drag a corner/edge handle to resize. Del deletes. C marks the frame as checked (also when it has no box).

    Strokes

    Each window = the frames of one folder (or all loaded images). Step with ←/→, toggle a stroke on the current frame with S or Enter, or double-click a thumbnail. C marks the window checked when done (a window with no stroke is saved only when checked).

    Open images to start. Press ? for help.
