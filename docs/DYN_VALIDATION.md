# DYN validation (DAYGEN v1 vs real DEV tracks)

Lane S3a, PLAN_TWIN §5 "DYN/DAYGEN". Generated by `scripts/dyn_validate.py` from `python -m twin.daygen --validate`; numbers below are copied from `docs/DYN_VALIDATION.json`.

## Rule

- Twin output goes through a camera model into tracks, the dataset's selection rule is applied, and the result is compared with the real DEV tracks on two statistics only: per-track floor speed and in-clip stroke interval (two-sample KS).
- A row passes when KS(twin, dev) <= 1.5 x KS(train, dev) (the train-vs-dev noise floor) AND KS(twin, dev) <= the 95th percentile of KS(bootstrap of train, dev) (500 bootstrap draws of the train clips with replacement, seed 20261018).
- Speed statistic (`twin.dynfit.observed_pieces` + `track_speed`, identical for twin, train and dev): track projected to the floor with CAL v1, rolling median of 7 samples, cut at the zone's traced floor polygon, at detection gaps > 1 s and at ID-switch jumps, pieces >= 2 s; speed = median over the piece of the floor displacement over 1 s.
- Stroke-interval statistic (`twin.dynfit.stroke_intervals`): consecutive strokes with no MISSING frame between them.

## Twin side

- World seeds 900-963 (dev range; NOT the dev worlds 0-3, which PLAN §4 generates only after PREREG v1), day 1, full 24 h days, 40 windows per zone and per press per day; windows kept: {'z9': 2560, 'z14': 2560, 'P9a': 2560, 'P9b': 2560}.
- Selection rule (event-selected clips): a mover window is anchored at a random arrival of the zone and reproduces its source clip (start = arrival - the unit's offset in its clip, length = that clip's duration, framing = that clip's framing); every mover in the zone during the window is filmed; the window is kept when the camera returns at least one track row. A press window takes a random TRAIN clip of the press (its frame grid and MISSING mask) at a uniform time of the generated day.
- Camera: **validation camera `twin.dynfit.PixelCamera`, not stat_v1** (twin/camera.py, lane S3b, was not merged when this ran). Floor -> pixels through the inverse CAL v1 H and k1 -> foot-point jitter and a two-state miss chain fitted on TRAIN tracks (`twin/dyn/valcam_v1.json`) -> back to the floor through the same CAL chain as the real tracks. Strokes: timing jitter fitted on train intervals, snapped to the clip's frame grid, dropped on MISSING frames. Re-run this report once stat_v1 lands.

## What this does and does not show

- **Floor coverage.** Only track rows on the zone's traced floor polygon (twin/site.yaml) enter the library and the statistics (the twin models only that floor; the rest is a wall). Train share of rows on it: z14/forklift 90%, z14/person 8%, z9/person 39%. Operators standing beside the presses mostly fall outside the traced camera-9 floor, and on camera 14 the floor polygon keeps very few person rows; S2 owns the polygon.
- **Zone-14 people.** 8 train and 4 dev person pieces: the speed row is uninformative at this n, and the twin's zone-14 people come from 9 resampling units only.
- **Camera-14 presses** never ran in a judged train clip (P4): P(running) is the Beta posterior of 0 running clips (mean P14a 0.028, P14b 0.024, P14c 0.022, P14d 0.024), the stroke period is the v0 prior (ASSUMED, grey); no dev strokes exist to compare.
- **In-clip pauses.** No complete in-clip interval longer than 1.5 x the period was seen on P9a or P9b, so the generator draws no observed micro-stops. The Kaplan-Meier mass beyond the last complete interval (P9a P(T > 2.91 s) = 0.235, P9b P(T > 2.5403 s) = 0.155) is censored at the clip end or at MISSING; it is left to the ASSUMED W-W3 long-stop process.
- **Stroke impulse** (FWHM of the ROI |flow_v| around a stroke): P9a 1.48 s, P9b 1.37 s; it reaches the +-0.8 s window, so the ROI moves through the whole cycle and this is not a resolved impulse.
- **Arrival rates are ASSUMED** and follow the plan's rule (train footage rate / recall): persons z9 1459/h, z14 111/h, forklifts z14 647/h, z9 0/h (no vehicles observed in zone-9 train clips). The clips are event-selected, so these reproduce the crowding the clips show, not an average hour. Person recall comes from the DEV labels (independent Claude-agent labels) against the DEV tracks; forklift recall from the P5 agent check (8/13).
- **Selection rule and the violation share.** The generated days draw unsafe-behaviour units at the ASSUMED share 0.05; the validation days draw them at the train footage share (0.706), because the dataset's clips are selected by behaviour class. The first trial run (16 worlds, assumed share) failed the zone-9 speed row; the share fix and 64 worlds (each day uses only one bright and one dim layout state per zone) were the only changes, no dynamics parameter was tuned on dev.
- **Not tested here:** arrival rates, staffing, stops, lighting / time of day and layout-state frequencies (all ASSUMED or not comparable on short clips), and paths beyond speed. Time of day is ASSUMED: night = the dim-luma share (0.329) of the 24 h, centred on midnight (P2: no timing signal).
